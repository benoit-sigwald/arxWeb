01 — The Problem

A PDF sent by email is a PDF lost

The daily reality for most high-end advisory firms and enterprises.

Your deal files leave via attachments

Plans, financials, pricing, photos: once sent, they get forwarded, archived, or shared — without your knowledge. You lose control over distribution and versions.

You don't know who is genuinely interested

Fifty emails sent, three replies. Impossible to distinguish a casual onlooker from a serious buyer who reopened your document six times this week.

Prospect contact data is scattered

Spreadsheets, inboxes, business cards. No usable unified database, no consent records — yet GDPR regulations still apply to your organization.

02 — The Solution

One Gate, One Database, One Dashboard

1 · Visitor presents themselves

Name, email, phone, organization, and visit purpose, recorded with timestamped GDPR consent. Custom bilingual form tailored to your brand.

2 · You approve from your phone

A instant notification arrives with the applicant's identity and location: two taps to Approve or Deny. Access opens within a second.

3 · Track who views what in real time

Every view becomes nominative: return frequency, pages viewed, time spent, location, and organization. Your follow-ups become laser-targeted.

4 · Your database stays 100% yours

All prospect data stored in a dedicated database hosted in France, exportable in one click. No US tool tracking, no data sharing.
