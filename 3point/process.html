Nightly trunk load planning

Operations · Do now
Problem Every night we run around 380 trunks between our 12 depots and the Lutterworth hub, and too many run part-empty. Average fill is 68% against an 80% target. My four planners build the plan in Excel from depot pre-advice that's wrong on about 15% of lanes, so we either send extra trailers 'just in case' or scramble for subbies at 11pm. With fuel and driver pay both up, this is the biggest cost leak I own.

Forecast the real pallet volume on each depot-to-hub lane by early evening and suggest a consolidated trunk plan, so my planners can cut part-empty trailers and last-minute subcontract bookings. Why: ~380 trunks/night, avg trailer fill 68% vs 80% target. Trunk cost ~£420/run, ~£40m/yr. Depot pre-advice wrong on ~15% of lanes by 18:00; plan built in Excel by 4 planners. Hub scan data is clean back to 2022.
Target: Within 12 months ! Cost of inaction is high — act now
