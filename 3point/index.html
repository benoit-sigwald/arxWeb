Objective Evaluation

The 3AIm Mathematical Scoring Engine

Eliminates subjective bias using a multi-criteria weighted scoring algorithm derived from 61-page 3AIm specifications and empirical industry benchmarks.

Overall Realisation Priority Score (\( S \))

Score = (0.35 × Impact) + (0.22 × DataReadiness) + (0.22 × Feasibility) + (0.21 × Confidence)

Where each dimension is evaluated on a normalized scale (1.0 – 5.0) validated against the Truthfulness Guard. Any score with a Truthfulness Confidence under 70% is automatically flagged for evidentiary re-verification.

35%
Business & Financial Impact
Revenue gain, cost takeout, loss avoidance
22%
Data & Org Readiness
Lineage, hygiene, API access, permissions
22%
Technical Feasibility
Model maturity, infra fit, compute requirements
21%
Truthfulness & Evidence
Factual backing, absence of unverified claims
