05 — How I work

Six steps, no mystery

The same method every time — that's what makes it repeatable.

  1. Listen first I spend the first days watching how your teams really work — where time is lost, what is repetitive, what people fear about AI.
  2. Find the champions A few people are already getting real value from AI. I find them and make them the visible example others want to follow.
  3. Set simple safety rules Which data may go into which tool — in plain words everyone remembers. Not a 50-page policy nobody opens.
  4. Pick quick wins Two or three low-risk, highly visible use cases, so the whole company sees a benefit within weeks.
  5. Train hands-on No slides. Everyone practises on their own real work — beginners gain confidence, advanced users become power users.
  6. Measure what matters Not attendance sheets: adoption, and one business number agreed up front.
Why hands-on is the whole secret. You cannot teach someone to swim with a slideshow. Confidence only comes from doing it on your own real work. I hold myself to the same rule: I build and run my own AI systems every day — I never recommend anything I haven't done myself.

The questions people ask me first

Straight answers, no sales talk.

Will my confidential data end up in a public AI tool?

No — that is exactly what I prevent. We agree which class of data may go into which tool, I set up the approved-tools list, and where needed I host the assistant on infrastructure in France that I operate myself. Your files never leave the perimeter we defined.

How long before we see something?

Two weeks for a diagnosis with a prioritised roadmap. First visible win for the teams within four to six weeks. A full adoption programme runs three to four months.

What if it doesn't work in our case?

Then I tell you during the diagnosis, before you commit to a programme. I would rather lose a mission than sell you an AI project that has no business case.

Do we need an IT department to work with you?

No. Half my clients have no CIO. I work directly with the business teams, and I handle the technical side myself — build, hosting, security, backups.

Are you going to replace our people with AI?

No. I make the people you already have faster on the work they already do. Every programme I run keeps a human in the loop on anything customer- or decision-facing.

How much does it cost?

A diagnosis is a fixed fee. Programmes are quoted after it, so the price matches the scope you actually chose. Hosted assistants are a monthly subscription — no servers to buy.
